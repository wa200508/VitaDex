## Exact proposed card text

### Giraffes

Scientific name: *Giraffa*. Revision 1; draft.

**Introduction:** Giraffes are tall African mammals. They browse on trees and shrubs, eating leaves and shoots. [Source 1](https://iucn.org/press-release/202508/four-giraffe-species-officially-recognised-major-conservation-reclassification) [Source 2](https://giraffeconservation.org/facts-about-giraffe/what-do-giraffe-eat/)

**Where it lives:** Parts of sub-Saharan Africa. [Source 1](https://giraffeconservation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GCF-Nature-Workbook-2022-LR-compressed.pdf)

**Role or use:** As plant-eating browsers, giraffes consume woody plants rather than mainly grazing on grass. [Source 1](https://giraffeconservation.org/facts-about-giraffe/what-do-giraffe-eat/)

**Scope note:** This card covers the genus. An IUCN specialist assessment recognized four giraffe species in 2025. [Source 1](https://iucn.org/press-release/202508/four-giraffe-species-officially-recognised-major-conservation-reclassification)

### Common sunflower

Scientific name: *Helianthus annuus*. Revision 1; draft.

**Introduction:** A sunflower head contains many tiny flowers. The central flowers develop into the familiar sunflower seeds. [Source 1](https://www.kew.org/plants/sunflower)

**Where it lives:** An annual plant native to the southwestern United States and Mexico; also widely cultivated. [Source 1](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/119003-2)

**Role or use:** Its seeds are used for food and oil, and processing leftovers can be used as feed. [Source 1](https://www.kew.org/plants/sunflower)

**Size:** Often around 2 metres tall; cultivated varieties differ. [Source 1](https://www.kew.org/plants/sunflower)

### Baker's yeast

Scientific name: *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*. Revision 1; draft.

**Introduction:** This yeast is a single-celled fungus. During fermentation it can turn sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide. [Source 1](https://doi.org/10.3934/microbiol.2020001)

**Where it lives:** Found in human-managed fermentations and natural settings including soil and plant surfaces. [Source 1](https://doi.org/10.3934/microbiol.2020001)

**Role or use:** Its fermentation activity is used in bread-making and other food and beverage production. [Source 1](https://doi.org/10.3934/microbiol.2020001)

**Scope note:** Its natural ecology is still studied; do not describe fruit as its one established natural niche. [Source 1](https://doi.org/10.1093/femsyr/fov009)

### E. coli

Scientific name: *Escherichia coli*. Revision 1; draft.

**Introduction:** E. coli are bacteria. Many kinds live harmlessly in intestines, while some kinds can cause illness. [Source 1](https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/about/index.html)

**Where it lives:** Found in intestines and also in settings such as water, food and the wider environment. [Source 1](https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/about/index.html)

**Role or use:** Harmless intestinal kinds can contribute to digestion and vitamin production. [Source 1](https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/about/index.html)
